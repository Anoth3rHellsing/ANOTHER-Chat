---
name: SoundCloud audio activation
description: Browser autoplay can produce SoundCloud widget activity without audible output
---

Treat SoundCloud's reported volume and play progress as widget state, not proof of audible browser output. Its official widget API uses integer volume 0–100, matching YouTube's API; a nominal volume-scale mismatch is not the explanation for silent starts. Even an effective iframe autoplay permission does not guarantee the browser will permit audible playback without a gesture. SoundCloud's play, pause, and seek callbacks also fire for script-driven changes; they are not reliable evidence that a listener manually changed the shared session.

**Why:** In an isolated Chromium check, the real component and official widget reported volume 60 and briefly advanced while the audio monitor stayed at zero and playback was then paused. A real click on the local activation action produced nonzero audio without changing the requested 60; silencing with volume 0 returned the output to zero. The widget has no getter for the browser's actual audio output. Chromium reported autoplay allowed in both parent and iframe, yet its default policy still blocked initial audible playback; with its autoplay restriction disabled, the official widget played without a gesture. The app additionally fed delayed widget PAUSE events back to the shared session, stopping playback even when the browser allowed it. Granting `encrypted-media` to the iframe removed a policy violation but did not make autoplay audible, so it was not kept.

**How to apply:** For synchronized embedded playback, distinguish player-reported volume from audible output, keep a visible per-device activation control even if the widget appears to be playing, and avoid echoing widget callbacks from scripted commands into the shared session. Do not widen iframe permissions based on console warnings alone; use a real browser/audio-output check to assess whether a permission change improves autoplay.
